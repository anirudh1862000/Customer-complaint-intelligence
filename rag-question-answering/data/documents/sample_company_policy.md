# Sample company policy (fictional demo)

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted to the manager at least five working days before the planned start date. Approval depends on team coverage.

## Remote work
Employees may request up to two remote-work days per week with manager approval. Roles requiring physical site presence may have different arrangements.

## Learning budget
Eligible employees may request reimbursement of up to 500 USD per year for approved job-related courses. Receipts and proof of completion are required.

This document is fictional and exists only to demonstrate document retrieval and source citations.
